# Datacenter Operations & Infrastructure Management

This directory serves as a comprehensive log and repository for all hands-on operational tasks, infrastructure maintenance, and physical engineering activities performed during my datacenter internship. 

The contents herein document the practical application of enterprise-grade hardware deployment, cable management topologies, and strict adherence to critical facility standard operating procedures (SOPs).

## 📌 Core Focus Areas

*   **Hardware Deployment:** Racking, stacking, and provisioning enterprise servers, switches, and power distribution units (PDUs).
*   **Infrastructure & Cabling:** Implementing structured cabling solutions (fiber optic and copper) following industry-standard color codes and bend-radius regulations.
*   **Asset Management:** Conducting physical inventory audits, component serialization tracking, and lifecycle management documentation.
*   **Facility Health & Safety:** Monitoring environmental controls (HVAC, hot/cold aisle containment) and executing routine preventative maintenance inspections.

## 🛠️ Key Technical Competencies Demonstrated

1. **Physical Layer Architecture:** Hands-on experience with server chassis configuration, rail kit installations, and component-level diagnostics (RAM, CPU, storage swaps).
2. **Cable Topologies:** Knowledge of Top-of-Rack (ToR) and Structured Cabling architectures to optimize airflow and maintain system redundancy.
3. **Safety & Compliance:** Strict adherence to Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) protection protocols and datacenter security compliance regulations.
